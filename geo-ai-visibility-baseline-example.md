# GEO / AI Visibility Baseline Example

### Sanitized Example: B2B SaaS / Automation Company

This example demonstrates how the **[GEO / LLM Visibility Framework](geo-llm-visibility-framework.md)** and **[GEO / AI Search Prompt Testing Template](geo-prompt-testing-template.md)** can be used to establish a repeatable AI-search visibility baseline.

All business names, URLs, competitor names, prompt results and scores in this example are fictionalized or generalized.

The purpose is to demonstrate:

- How to define an AI visibility baseline
- How to build a prompt test set
- How to compare multiple AI platforms
- How to record brand mentions and citations
- How to assess entity accuracy
- How to identify competitor and citation gaps
- How to prioritize improvements
- How to repeat testing over time

## 2. Baseline Objective

The primary objective was to understand:

```text
Can AI-powered discovery systems:

1. Identify the company correctly?
2. Associate it with the intended service category?
3. Include it in category and recommendation prompts?
4. Cite owned or third-party sources?
5. Describe its services accurately?
6. Surface competitors more consistently?
7. Reveal information gaps we can improve?
```

The baseline was designed as a **repeatable measurement system**, not as an attempt to identify a fixed AI ranking position.

### Main Accuracy Issues

1. The company was sometimes described too broadly as a "digital marketing agency."
2. CRM automation was not always identified as a core capability.
3. Geographic coverage was inconsistently represented.
4. Leadership information was missing in several responses.
5. Some AI responses emphasized older service descriptions.

# 14. Entity Clarity Gap

The owned website used multiple descriptions:

```text
AI Revenue Growth Company

Digital Marketing Agency

CRM Automation Company

AI Automation Solutions

SEO & Marketing Automation Agency
```

These descriptions were individually valid but created inconsistency around the primary entity category.

### Recommendation

Adopt a clearer primary description across high-authority pages.

Example:

> CRM, AI Automation & Search Growth Systems for Businesses

Then use secondary descriptions for supporting capabilities.

The objective is not to force identical wording everywhere.

The objective is to reduce ambiguity around the primary entity.

# 16. Citation Gap Analysis

Competitor citations often came from:

- Technology publications
- Industry directories
- Review platforms
- Partner pages
- Product comparison sites

The example company relied more heavily on:

- Its own website
- Social profiles
- General business directories

### Recommendation

Strengthen:

```text
Owned Expertise
        +
Relevant Third-Party Authority
        +
Original Resources
        +
Industry Mentions
```

The goal is not mass link building.

The goal is to build **credible independent corroboration**.

---

# 17. Passage-Level Content Analysis

Some important service pages used generic copy such as:

```text
We help businesses grow using cutting-edge technology and automation.
```

This is difficult to interpret precisely.

### Improved Example

```text
Our CRM automation services connect lead forms, WhatsApp,
email, calendars and sales pipelines so incoming enquiries
can be captured, qualified, assigned and followed up automatically.
```

The second version is more specific and easier for both users and machines to understand.

---

# 18. Structured Information Findings

Strong pages tended to include:

- Clear headings
- Service definitions
- Use cases
- Step-by-step workflows
- FAQs
- Tables
- Named tools/integrations
- Clear CTAs

Weak pages tended to rely on:

- Broad marketing language
- Short sections
- Unclear service categories
- Limited evidence

### Recommendation

Improve important pages using:

```text
Definition
↓
Problem
↓
Solution
↓
Workflow
↓
Use Cases
↓
Evidence
↓
FAQs
↓
Conversion
```

# 25. What This Example Demonstrates

A GEO baseline should not simply ask:

> Does ChatGPT mention my brand?

A stronger assessment asks:

```text
Is the brand understood?
↓
Is the category association correct?
↓
Does it appear in relevant prompts?
↓
Is it recommended?
↓
Is it cited?
↓
Are the facts accurate?
↓
Which competitors appear?
↓
Which sources are used?
↓
What should we improve?
```

That creates a repeatable **AI visibility measurement system**.
